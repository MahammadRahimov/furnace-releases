Docker image only: the image is built on Debian 12 (debian:12-slim) and includes Debian packages, for example
python3, nginx, keepalived, openssl, curl and sqlite3. Their licenses are in /usr/share/doc/<package>/copyright
inside the image. Their source code is on https://sources.debian.org (the versions are listed by
`dpkg -l` inside the image).

On a server installed from the .deb or .rpm package, Furnace uses the system's own programs (python3, nginx,
keepalived and others). They are not part of Furnace and come with the system's own licenses.
